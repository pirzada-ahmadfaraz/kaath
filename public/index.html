Learn it in a minute

  1. Four players, two teams. Your partner sits opposite you.
  2. Call the rang. One player gets 5 cards first and chooses any suit (hearts, spades, diamonds or clubs) as the trump.
  3. Deal the rest. Everyone ends up with 13 cards.
  4. Follow suit. If you can't, play any card. A rang card beats everything else.
  5. Win 7 tricks to take the hand. Win the first 7 in a row and it's a Court, worth triple.
Rang is ♠

Three hearts are led. The 4th player has no hearts, plays the 2♠ and wins the trick, even over the Ace.
